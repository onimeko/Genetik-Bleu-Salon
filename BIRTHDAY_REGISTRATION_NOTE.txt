GENETIK BLEU — OPTIONAL BIRTHDAY REGISTRATION UPDATE

What changed:
- Client Registration now has a polished OPTIONAL birthday section.
- Only month + day are collected. No birth year is requested or stored.
- Day choices update automatically based on the selected month, including February 29.
- Birthday is stored as birthdayMonth and birthdayDay only.
- If birthday is left blank, both stored values are 0.
- Future birthday automation can query the two numeric fields directly, so no redundant birthday key is stored.
- Admin client cards show a birthday chip when a birthday is on file.
- Clients with both a birthday and marketing consent are labeled "Rewards eligible".
- Client CSV export includes Birthday, a computed Birthday Key, and Birthday Marketing Eligible.
- Privacy Policy explains the optional birthday field and the marketing-consent boundary.
- Firestore Rules validate the new birthday fields.
- No birthday messages or rewards are sent yet. This update only collects and displays the data.
