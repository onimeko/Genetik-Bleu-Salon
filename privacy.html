Privacy Policy

How we use the information you share when you register as a salon client. Last updated September 2026.

Information we collect

When you register, we collect your first and last name, email address, phone number, communication preference and any consent you choose to give. We also keep consent dates and appointment details that the salon enters in its private schedule.

How we use it

We use this information to manage the client directory and appointments and, once enabled, to send the appointment updates you chose. Optional promotional emails are kept separate from appointment updates. We do not send promotional texts through this registration form.

Who can access it

Approved salon administrators can access the private client directory. We use Firebase to store client and appointment records. When email and texting are activated, the relevant delivery providers will process the contact information needed to deliver your chosen updates. We do not sell personal information or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Your choices

Appointment updates are optional. You can choose email, text, both, or no automated updates. If you consent to text updates, message frequency depends on your appointments, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to the salon's messaging number to stop texts, or HELP for help, once SMS service is active. Contact Marni to change your preferences, correct information, request a copy, or ask for deletion. Legal retention obligations may apply. Consent to messages is not a condition of receiving salon services.

Questions and requests

Call Genetik Bleu Salon at (773) 359-2491 during operating hours or use the contact options on our website.
