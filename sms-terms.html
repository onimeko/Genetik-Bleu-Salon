Text Messaging Terms

Optional appointment-related SMS program. Last updated September 2026.

Program and messages

Genetik Bleu Salon may send appointment confirmations, reminders, rescheduling updates and cancellation notices to clients who separately opt in to appointment text messages on our client registration form. We do not enroll you in promotional text messages through this form. Automated texting is not active yet.

Consent and frequency

Text updates are optional, and consent is not a condition of salon services or purchase. The number of messages varies with your appointments. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to the number that sends your appointment texts to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for assistance, or call (773) 359-2491 during salon hours. You may also contact Marni to request a preference change. These reply options will be available once our texting program is activated.

Delivery and privacy

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Please review our Privacy Policy for details about how we handle personal information and messaging consent.
