GENETIK BLEU — CLIENT PDF EXPORT

Changed:
- Existing client export is now clearly labeled "Export CSV".
- Added a separate "Export PDF" button.
- PDF is generated directly in the browser; client data is not sent to a PDF service.
- PDF is formatted as an easy-to-read client directory with:
  name, phone, email, birthday, marketing status, registration date,
  birthday reward details (when enabled), and client notes.
- PDF automatically paginates for large client lists.

No Firebase rules, Firestore data, registration logic, appointment logic,
or email automation were changed.
