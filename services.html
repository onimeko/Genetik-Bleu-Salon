Your Visit

What to Expect

Every appointment is customized, but your visit will generally follow this thoughtful salon process.

01

Consultation

Your stylist begins with a focused conversation about your hair history, goals, maintenance routine, and desired result.

02

Cleanse + Condition

When appropriate for your service, the hair is cleansed and conditioned with products selected for your texture, condition, and service needs.

03

Customized Service

Your cut, color, treatment, or chemical service is performed with thoughtful adjustments made for your hair and desired outcome.

04

Finish + Care Plan

Your look is refined and finished, followed by maintenance guidance and recommendations to help preserve your results at home.
