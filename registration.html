Genetik Bleu Client Directory

Client registration.

All new and existing clients who have not registered with Genetik Bleu Salon can do so before receiving their service. Registration helps maintain an accurate client directory, keeps contact information current, and sends appointment reminders and other important salon communications.

Appointments are scheduled directly with the stylist. Services are not booked through this website. Please call during operating hours or message the stylist via the provided Instagram to schedule an appointment.
